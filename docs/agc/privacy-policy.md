## 一、我们收集什么？

什么都不收集。

日新没有账号系统，没有注册登录，没有云端服务器，不内嵌任何广告 SDK 或统计／分析 SDK。应用不会把你的任何数据发送到网络。

## 二、数据存在哪里？

你的所有数据——习惯、打卡记录、笔记、待办、专注记录、设置——只保存在你自己设备上的应用数据目录中（本地 Hive 数据库，位于应用文档目录）。

数据不同步、不上传、不与任何第三方共享。

## 三、权限说明

| 权限 | 用途 |
|---|---|
| `ohos.permission.INTERNET` | 系统普通权限（安装即授予，无需用户确认）。应用核心功能完全离线，应用自身不通过网络传输你的数据；该权限为应用运行框架所需。 |
| `ohos.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | 仅用于发送你为习惯设置的本地提醒通知。通知在设备本地排定，内容不经过任何服务器；不用于营销推送。 |

你可以拒绝通知权限；拒绝后提醒不再弹出，应用的其他功能不受影响。

## 四、备份与删除

- 你可以随时在应用内把全部数据导出为备份文件（zip / JSON），自行保存或迁移到新设备。
- 卸载应用即删除设备上的全部本地数据。开发者无法帮你找回，因为开发者从来收不到你的数据。

## 五、未成年人

本应用不收集任何用户信息，自然也不收集未成年人的信息。

## 六、政策变更

如本政策发生变化，会在本页面更新内容并注明日期。

# Privacy Policy (English)

Renew does not collect anything.

- **Last updated:** `2026-11-10`
- **Developer:** chenbf (individual developer) · Contact: `<PRIVACY-CONTACT>`
- **Source code:** `https://github.com/nanhaizhiyun/renew-harmonyos` — a HarmonyOS adaptation of [Streak](https://github.com/InlitX/streak) by InlitX, GNU GPLv3.

**Data collected:** none. Renew has no accounts, no sign-up, no cloud servers, and embeds no ad or analytics SDKs. The app never sends your data over the network.

**Storage:** all your data — habits, check-ins, notes, todos, focus sessions, settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's local documents directory (a local Hive database). Nothing syncs, uploads, or is shared with third parties.

**Permissions:**

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `ohos.permission.INTERNET` | A normal, install-time permission required by the app runtime. Renew's core features are fully offline; the app itself does not transmit your data over the network. |
| `ohos.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Used only to deliver the local reminders you set for your habits. Notifications are scheduled on-device and never pass through a server; they are never used for marketing. |

You can deny the notification permission; reminders stop, and every other feature keeps working.

**Backup and deletion:** export a full backup (zip / JSON) from within the app at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data; the developer cannot recover it, because the developer never receives it.

**Children:** the app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy are published on this page with an updated date.
